## Start a new Claude Code session

Sessions that were already running do not have the hooks yet. Start a new one:

1. Open a terminal in this window.
2. Run `claude`.
3. Ask for something that takes a moment, then switch to another app.

You will hear a sound when Claude needs you.

**Not hearing it for quick replies?** That is on purpose. While you are looking at this
window, turns shorter than 10 seconds stay quiet. Change **Min Turn Seconds** in the
settings to adjust it.

The bell in the status bar is your menu: mute, snooze, sounds and settings.
